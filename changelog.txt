v.12.4.0

General:
	- REMOVED SUPPORT FOR B41. Legacy version can be found [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773982162]here[/url]. You do not lose any progress even if you load save without mod, just gotta re-enable b41 version if you are staying on b41.
	- Added the Injuries System for dynamic Thin Skinned and Thick Skinned progression. Current injuries increase its counter every ten minutes and fortify the character over time, while injury-free checks decay it; every contribution and the maximum counter are configurable in sandbox settings.
	- Added an Injuries System progress bar to the Non-permanent Traits UI.
	- Removed Thin Skinned and Thick Skinned from the Weight System. The Weight System now manages only Slow Healer and Fast Healer.
Fixes:
	- Fixed pettingAnimal event after 42.20 change.

description=[h1]Welcome to the world of Evolving Traits[/h1]
description=This mod adds a lot of traits (65). Additionally, this mod allows you to get really ripped in PZ and see an actual character progression besides getting your skills from lvl 0 to lvl10. You'll be able to purge the majority of negative traits and earn a lot of positive ones (but also earn some negative ones as well). This would help you to go from your average Joe to actual GigaChad in-game. You repair a lot of cars? You get better at it. You train yourself to take long-range kills with proper rifles? Why wouldn't it warranty getting Eagle-Eyed? You pick up thousands of plants through your lifetime and still can't call yourself Herbalist? Yea, that doesn't sound right to me. You maintain great sleeping schedule but for some reason you still need same amount of sleep as when you were sleeping whenever? Not anymore. Welcome to the Dynamic Traits World, ladies and gentlemen!
description=
description=[h1]Features[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Extensive UI with all needed mod information in-game! No more needing 3 screens to play PZ and keep track of your progress. Just hover over UI elements in new UI for all needed info.
description=[*]65 new traits.
description=[*][h3][b]FULL sandbox customization[/b]. Choose which perks you want to be dynamic, choose how fast you get them, choose how fast you lose them, customize anything with sandbox options. Nothing is forced upon player, if you like 1% of the mod, you can literally set it up so you get to use that 1% and nothing else.[/h3]
description=[*]Advanced systems that affect a lot of traits. Affinity system, that allows you to stick to your starting traits better. Delayed traits system, that allows you to get/remove traits with random delay.
description=[*]Mark dynamic traits in character creation with the help of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3766701843]Mark Dynamic Traits Framework (MDTF)[/url]
description=[*]For new traits, extensive sandbox customization is also present.
description=[*]Optional [b]Trait Sandbox[/b] submod: enable/disable ETW-added traits and change their point values. Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3378285185]Starlit Library[/url].
description=[*]Client-sided configuration available (Exc -> Options -> Mod Options).
description=[*]Legacy (b41) version can be found [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773982162]here[/url].
description=[/list]
